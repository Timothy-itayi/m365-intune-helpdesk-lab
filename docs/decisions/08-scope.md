# Decisions: Scope

Previous: [07-intune.md](07-intune.md)

## Decisions

- Focus the lab on the Microsoft 365 side: tenant, groups, PowerShell automation, MFA and Conditional Access, and Intune policy configuration (Phases 1 to 5).
- Keep the two setup incident reports and write six runbooks from the steps run in the lab ([README.md](../runbooks/README.md)).
- Treat the tenant as a temporary lab with fictional users. The screenshots and write-ups are the record.

## Trade-offs

- A temporary tenant means nothing can be re-checked later, so each step is written up with its screenshots as it is done.
